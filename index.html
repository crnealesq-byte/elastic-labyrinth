What is real in this model?

The network is the tesseract graph Q₄: 16 vertices and 32 edges. A gate connects addresses that differ by one bit. Openings below 10% block a link; smaller usable openings increase its modeled delay. The rotating view is a projection, not a physical fourth dimension.

The inside view is a conceptual room with four portals, one per graph neighbor. Its walls are a navigation metaphor. Gates are symmetric, and closing a link immediately drops any message traversing it. Routing is recalculated at each arrival. Changing a destination does not alter messages already sent.

Sound is synthesized locally: one voice per edge direction. The learning experiment estimates synthetic corridor delays with sample averages, then uses shortest-cost paths. It is a classical routing experiment—not quantum entanglement, a fusion simulation, or a claim of AI advantage. Display animation timing is separate from benchmark delay units.

Keyboard: Tab to controls; Enter/Space to activate. In Inside view, keys 1–4 enter the X/Y/Z/W portals when focus is not in an input. Sound starts only on request. No accounts, uploads, analytics, or external libraries.
